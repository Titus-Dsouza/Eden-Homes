## Workflow

1. **Explore Services** – Visitors explore the 1 BHK, 2 BHK, and boating pages.
2. **Enter Details** – Visitors complete the appropriate website form; browser
   validation checks required fields and date relationships.
3. **Prepare Message** – When the owner-confirmed destination is configured,
   the browser opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message; the visitor must
   manually press Send. The current destination is unset, so the form displays
   a clear configuration error instead.
4. **Owner Review** – The owner reviews the WhatsApp message and responds
   directly.

The WhatsApp destination number must be confirmed by the owner before messages
can be generated. The initial version does not save enquiry details,
create enquiry history, or provide an owner dashboard.

**Note:** Submitting a form or opening WhatsApp does not confirm a booking.
Final confirmation is provided by the owner.
